Good to know

FAQ

✦
Can I bring a plus one?

We have reserved seats for each guest named on your invitation. Kindly let us know through the RSVP form.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones, but our celebration will be adults-only, except for children in the entourage.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, free parking is available at both the ceremony and reception venues.

Can we take photos during the ceremony?

We're having an unplugged ceremony. Please keep phones away and let our photographers capture the moment. Feel free to snap away at the reception!

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive at least 30 minutes before the ceremony starts.
